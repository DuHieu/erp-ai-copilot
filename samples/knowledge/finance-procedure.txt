ACCOUNTS RECEIVABLE COLLECTION PROCEDURE
Document Code: SOP-FIN-004
Effective Date: July 1, 2026
Version: 3.2

1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE
This document defines the standard operating procedure for monitoring, following up, and collecting outstanding customer receivables across all corporate divisions.

2. OVERDUE AGEING ESCALATION MATRIX
- 1 to 7 Days Overdue: Automated email payment reminder sent to primary customer contact.
- 8 to 14 Days Overdue: Phone call by Accounts Receivable specialist. Re-send invoice and statement of account.
- 15 to 30 Days Overdue: Issue formal Notice of Default signed by Credit Control Manager. Temporarily hold new sales orders.
- Over 30 Days Overdue: Escalate to Legal and Chief Financial Officer for debt recovery or legal collection action.

3. APPROVAL AUTHORITY FOR CREDIT LIMIT EXTENSION
- Up to $50,000 USD: Finance Manager Approval.
- $50,001 to $200,000 USD: Chief Financial Officer Approval.
- Above $200,000 USD: Board of Directors Approval.
