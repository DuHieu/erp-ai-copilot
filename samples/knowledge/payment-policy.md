# Corporate Payment & Disbursement Policy

## Section 1: Payment Voucher Verification
All payment requests must be accompanied by three-way matching verification:
1. Approved Purchase Order (PO)
2. Verified Goods Received Note (GRN)
3. Valid Tax Invoice issued by vendor

## Section 2: Approval Thresholds
- **Below 10,000,000 VND**: Department Manager
- **10,000,001 to 100,000,000 VND**: Financial Controller
- **Above 100,000,000 VND**: General Director / CEO Signature Required

## Section 3: Payment Cut-off Dates
Payment runs are executed bi-weekly on **1st and 15th** of each month. Emergency disbursements require CFO authorization.
