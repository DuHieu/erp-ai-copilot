PAYMENT APPROVAL POLICY
Document Code: FIN-POL-001
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: January 1, 2026

SECTION 1: PAYMENT APPROVAL AUTHORITY

All corporate payments and disbursements must be approved according to the
following authority matrix:

1.1 Payments up to 50,000,000 VND:
    Approved by: Department Manager

1.2 Payments from 50,000,001 VND to 100,000,000 VND:
    Approved by: Financial Controller

1.3 Payments above 100,000,000 VND:
    Approved by: General Director (Chief Executive Officer)

NOTE: This is Policy Version 1.0. Please verify against the latest issued version.

SECTION 2: EMERGENCY PAYMENTS

Emergency disbursements outside the regular payment cycle require
Chief Financial Officer (CFO) authorization regardless of amount.
