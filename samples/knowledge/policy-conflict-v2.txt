PAYMENT APPROVAL POLICY — REVISED
Document Code: FIN-POL-001-REV
Version: 2.0
Effective Date: July 1, 2026

SECTION 1: REVISED PAYMENT APPROVAL AUTHORITY

Following the annual policy review, payment approval thresholds have been
updated as follows:

1.1 Payments up to 50,000,000 VND:
    Approved by: Department Manager

1.2 Payments from 50,000,001 VND to 200,000,000 VND:
    Approved by: Financial Controller

1.3 Payments above 200,000,000 VND:
    Approved by: General Director (Chief Executive Officer)

NOTE: This revised policy (Version 2.0) supersedes Version 1.0 dated January 1, 2026.
If the ERP system still reflects the old threshold of 100,000,000 VND,
please contact Finance Operations to update the approval workflow.

SECTION 2: EMERGENCY PAYMENTS

Emergency disbursements outside the regular payment cycle require
Chief Financial Officer (CFO) authorization regardless of amount.
